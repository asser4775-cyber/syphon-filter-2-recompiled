# Release media checklist

Keep media outside Git. Upload it directly to Reddit and/or a video host; this
repository does not track screenshots, video, audio, saves, or game assets.

## Video

Record one mostly uncut 60–90 second clip:

1. cold launch and the SCUS-94451 disc picker (hide personal paths);
2. title/menu transition;
3. Mission 1 outdoor gameplay showing the expanded 16:9 view;
4. mouse chase camera and held-L1 manual aim;
5. a close geometry shot where PGXP stability is visible;
6. controller input if it can be shown without interrupting the clip.

Capture at 1080p60 or better for upload quality, but label the video clearly:
the renderer may present at the display refresh while the game's world update
remains the authentic 20 Hz. Do not title or caption it as a 60 FPS mod.

## Screenshots

Capture four to six clean images:

- title or main menu;
- FMV/briefing contained at its authored aspect;
- wide Mission 1 outdoor scene;
- close actor/environment geometry showing PGXP;
- manual-aim view with HUD;
- memory-card or pause screen if visually correct.

Optional: one clearly labelled 4:3 versus 16:9 comparison from the same scene.
Do not stretch the 4:3 half or imply that additional side visibility exists
where it does not.

## Privacy and accuracy pass

- Hide usernames, drive letters, disc filenames, debug ports, and local paths.
- Do not show BIOS/disc management tools or copyrighted file listings.
- Do not upload saves, captures, generated code, or runtime dumps.
- Disable performance overlays unless the numbers are part of a labelled test.
- State `v0.1.0-alpha`, SCUS-94451, partial native overlay coverage, and the
  absence of high-refresh interpolation in the video description.
